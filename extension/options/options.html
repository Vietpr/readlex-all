Lookup popup

AI explanations

After you save a word, Gemini explains what it means in the sentence you were reading. AI never runs on hover.

Gemini key is working

The key is encrypted and stored on the server, and used by both the extension and the review web app. Each person uses their own key.

The key is stored only in this browser. Get a free key at Google AI Studio.

If you are not sure, keep gemini-2.5-flash.

Disabled sites

ReadLex does not run on these sites. One domain per line, for example mail.google.com. You can also turn a site on or off in the extension popup.

Advanced

Clear translation cache Removes cached translations. Your vocabulary stays as it is.
Delete all data Deletes all vocabulary, sentences and lookup history on this device. This cannot be undone, so back up first.
